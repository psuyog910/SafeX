How SafeX works

The whole design in one page.

Encryption keys come from your passkey

When you save an entry, SafeX generates a 32-byte random salt, derives a 256-bit key from your passkey and that salt using PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 with 200,000 iterations, and encrypts the password with Fernet. Only the ciphertext and the salt are stored. Your passkey is never written anywhere.

Your login is separate from your passkeys

Account passwords are hashed with a salted, iterated algorithm — they are never stored in readable form. Account access and vault decryption are two different locks, so a compromised account password alone does not open your entries.

Each entry is checked for ownership

Every decrypt, update and delete verifies the entry belongs to the signed-in account before touching the row. Guessing an entry id from another account returns a refusal, not data.

What SafeX cannot do

It cannot reset a forgotten passkey, recover a lost password, or read your plaintext. That is the point — but it means you are responsible for remembering your passkeys. A password manager without a master-key escape hatch by design.
