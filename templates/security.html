What protects this vault

The current security posture.

Vault keys stretched hard

PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 at 200,000 iterations with a unique 32-byte random salt per item. Changing a password or updating an entry rotates the salt, so old ciphertext keys are worthless.

Account passwords hashed

Your login is stored as a salted, iterated hash and is never recoverable. Login failures return one identical message, so nobody can probe for valid usernames.

Requests verified and throttled

Every state-changing form carries a CSRF token, and sign-in, sign-up and breach lookups are rate limited per address. Responses carry HSTS, CSP, frame and content-type protections.

Ownership enforced server-side

Reads, updates, deletes and reveals all confirm the item belongs to the signed-in account before touching the row, and the clipboard clears itself 30 seconds after a copy.

What it still cannot do

Recover a lost vault key, or read your plaintext. There is no reset by design. A vault key that is lost is a vault entry that is gone.
