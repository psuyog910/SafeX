Your passwords.

SafeX encrypts every password with a passkey only you know, using PBKDF2 with a unique random salt. We never see it, we never store it, we cannot recover it.

200,000 PBKDF2 rounds Unique salt per entry No passkey recovery

Your passkey is the whole security model

Every entry is encrypted client-side-style with a key derived from your passkey and its own random salt. A leaked database dump gives an attacker ciphertext and nothing else.

Your account password is hashed, not stored

Login credentials go through a salted, iterated hash. Nobody, including the server operator, can read your account password back out of the database.

Entries are scoped to your account

Every read, update and delete checks ownership on the server. One account can never reach another's vault, even by guessing an entry id.
